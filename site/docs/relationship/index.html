    VXP Relationship

    The authorized agreement within which a company can start a request toward a person's agent, and how it is born, used, limited and revoked.

    On this page

    • Why it exists
    • How it is born
    • How the company uses it
    • Limits and revocation
    • Agents without an inbox
    • Status

    Why it exists#

    Some work cannot wait for the person's agent to ask. A guest has not arrived and the hotel must decide whether to hold the room; a delivery has to move; a suspicious order needs a yes or a no. VXP Relationship lets the company reach the person's agent for that, and only within an agreement the person approved first.

    How it is born#

    The agent asks for the relationship in the same consent it asks for authority, with an authorization_details entry of type https://vxpprotocol.org/rar/relationship (R1):

    authorization_details · relationshipjson
    { "type": "https://vxpprotocol.org/rar/relationship",
  "actions": ["despacho.proponer_cambio"],
  "identifier": "relationship",
  "limits": { "max_per_day": 2, "duration_seconds": 3600 } }
    • The actions must be among those the company publishes in vxp_relationship.company_initiated_actions. The company caps the limits at its own maximums (R1).
    • The agent declares an inbox in its Client ID Metadata Document, on the same origin as its client_id (R2).
    • The consent screen shows the relationship in business language, with its own checkbox. Unchecked, no relationship is created (R3).
    • On approval, the token response carries relationships[] and a signed agreement (vxp-relationship+jwt), with the company's relationship key anchored to its issuer (R4, R5).

  "relationship_id": "rel_X9sUhyHhjsLp",

    How the company uses it#

    Before it signs or sends anything, the company checks that (R6):

    • the relationship and the delegation that created it are still in force;
    • the action is in the agreement;
    • the daily limit is not exceeded;
    • the resource belongs to the same customer.

    The message is a vxp-company-request+jwt delivered to the agent's inbox (R7). There are two kinds:

    • A proposal (R8): it creates a normal operation, with terms signed by the company and its own VXP Proof chain. Proposing never executes. The agent accepts with the person's approval, and the company checks the relationship again at that moment (R9).
    • A closed question (R17): for example «hold or release the booking». The agent answers once, with one of the options, before it expires, and gets a signed receipt (vxp-reply-receipt+jwt).

    Limits and revocation#

    • The person, the company or the agent can revoke. Revoking the delegation revokes the relationship. Once it is revoked or expired, nothing goes through (R10).
    • The agent reads or revokes its relationship with GET and DELETE /v1/relationships/{id} (R11). A late answer to a question does not count after revocation.
    • The relationship never replaces the company's authorization: inside an agreement, each request still passes the company's own rules and the operation requirements (O1–O7).

    Agents without an inbox#

    An agent that only speaks Poppy and has no HTTP inbox can still hold a relationship. It is asked for, received and answered inside its Poppy conversation, without changing the Poppy contract (R12–R16). See Interoperability.

    Status#

    Experimental. The HTTP inbox has been tested with agents written by SOVRAS. The relationship over a Poppy conversation was used once by a live agent, on 21 October 2026. No third-party agent exposes an inbox yet.
