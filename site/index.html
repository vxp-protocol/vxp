Open protocol · draft 0.1

What the agent asked. What the person approved. What the system did.

VXP is an open protocol for work that personal agents do for people inside companies. It has two public components: VXP Proof, signed evidence of what was asked, approved, executed and then read in the company's own system, and VXP Relationship, the agreement the person authorized, within which either side can start a request.

Read the specification Verify a proof

Native profile: experimentalInteroperates with Poppy draft 0.2 and PACT 1.0Apache 2.0

Where it comes from

Companies already do this work. Now agents ask for it.

Every day, companies sell, book, change deliveries and handle incidents inside their own systems. More and more often the request comes from a person's own agent, which the company did not build and whose conversation it cannot see. For the company to act on that request, it needs a relationship the person authorized; for anyone to trust the result, it has to be verifiable afterwards. After each operation, the same questions come back:

  1. 1Who asked, and on whose behalf?
  2. 2Which exact terms did the person approve, and through which channel?
  3. 3What ran, and how many times?
  4. 4What did the company's system record afterwards?

VXP was created by SOVRAS, which builds agents that change orders, deliveries and reservations inside companies' systems. SOVRAS's reference implementation is the first implementation of VXP.

    Two questions
    
			
    What it allows, and what it depends on
    
		
    
		
    
			
    
				
    What can a personal agent do with a company that implements VXP?
    
				
      
					
    • Find the company and read what it offers.
      • 
					
    • Identify itself and say which person it acts for.
      • 
					
    • Receive permission for one specific operation, and ask for more when it needs it.
      • 
					
    • Ask, negotiate and receive a proposal with exact terms.
      • 
					
    • Have the person approve those terms, then see the operation run once.
      • 
					
    • Receive a signed record of what the company's system shows afterwards.
      • 
					
    • Answer when the company asks something within an agreement the person approved.
      • 
				
    
			
    
			
    
				
    Can a company implement VXP without Poppy, PACT or the SOVRAS platform?
    
				
    
					
    What the specification allows
    Yes. A company can implement VXP with the native profile alone: discovery, authorization, operations, VXP Proof and VXP Relationship. Nothing in the two components or in the operation requirements depends on Poppy or PACT, and no SOVRAS service is needed (specification §8, native-only gateway).
    
					
    What the native implementation has today
    The reference implementation runs as a native-only gateway, with both adapters switched off. A test runs the whole cycle with an agent that signs with plain node:crypto, and checks the proof with the independent verifier anchored by the issuer alone. It is experimental: tested, and no agent outside SOVRAS has used it yet.
    
					
    Where Poppy and PACT fit
    They are optional adapters, for agents that already speak those protocols. A company can publish them next to the native profile, on the same issuer.
    
				
    
			
    
		
    
	
    

    


    
	
    
		
    
			
    What VXP defines
    
			
    Two public components
    
		
    
		
    
			
    
				
    VXP Proof
    
				
    A chain of signed statements that binds, by digest, the request, the exact terms of each revision, the approval and its method, the execution, what the company's system showed afterwards and the declared result. When the outcome is unknown, the chain says unknown. Proof is not an execution engine: it records what was asked, approved, executed and observed.
    
				
    The verifier derives the level of evidence. Today it reports one root of trust: the operator.
    
			
    
			
    
				
    VXP Relationship
    
				
    The agreement the person authorized between their agent and a company: both parties, the actions allowed in each direction, a daily limit, validity and revocation. Within it, the company can start a request toward the agent, such as keeping or releasing a booking. Relationship does not replace the company's authorization: each request still passes the company's own rules.
    
				
    Signed agreement vxp-relationship+jwt, signed company request, signed reply receipt.
    
			
    
		
    
		
    VXP also states operation requirements O1 to O7: the behaviour every implementation shows where a request becomes an operation, such as executing only the exact revision that was approved, and only once. They define no interface. How an implementation enforces them is its own decision, and VXP Proof leaves the evidence to check them afterwards.

    Its own profile, and adapters for interoperability
    
			
    The native profile is VXP's own form. For agents that already speak Poppy or PACT, a company can publish those adapters next to it, on the same issuer.

    21 October 2026
    
			
    How personal agents took part
    
			
    These operations ran in SOVRAS's production with customers, first on SOVRAS's own resources. In the evidence, the companies are configured as test environments, and payments and the WhatsApp line are simulated. Personal agents came in through the Poppy adapter, among them Nova AI, SOVRAS's personal agent in beta, which works on the person's side: the person talks to it on WhatsApp, and Nova goes to the company through Poppy. A third-party web agent, Grok, came in through the companies' web pages.
