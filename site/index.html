Open protocol · draft 0.1

Companies book, sell, change and fix things for people every day. Now the request comes from the person's own agent.

VXP is an open protocol for that work. The person authorizes what their agent may do with the company, the company does it in its own systems under its own rules, and both keep signed evidence that anyone can check afterwards.

1 · The work

It starts with something a company has to resolve

A booking, a sale, a change, a request or a problem. The company already knows how to do it, in its own systems and under its own rules. What changes is who asks.

  • Retail

    Move the delivery of an order to another day

    Inside the company: check the route and the stock, change the date in the order system.

  • Hotels

    Book two nights, or hold a booking for a late arrival

    Inside the company: quote the current rate, hold the room, record the guarantee.

  • Distribution

    Buy thirty units at the volume price

    Inside the company: apply the discount policy, issue the quote, create the order.

  • Healthcare

    Reschedule an appointment

    Inside the company: find a free slot with the same professional and update the schedule.

  • Utilities and telecom

    Report an outage at home and follow it up

    Inside the company: open the ticket, assign a visit, report each change of state.

  • Insurance

    Open a claim after an accident

    Inside the company: check the policy, register the claim, ask for what is missing.

2 · Who asks

More and more, it is the person's own agent

The person talks to their agent, on the phone or in a chat. The agent goes to the company to ask for something, to pick up a request already under way, or to answer when the company needs a decision. The company did not build that agent and cannot see its conversation.

Before it acts, the company needs four answers, and so does anyone who checks the work later:

  1. 1Who asks, and on whose behalf?
  2. 2What may that agent do here, and until when?
  3. 3Which exact terms were accepted, and how?
  4. 4What ran, and what did the company's system show afterwards?

3 · What VXP adds

A relationship, exact conditions and evidence

VXP does not replace how the company works. It gives the agent and the company a common way to agree on what may be done, to do it exactly once, and to prove it.

  1. Relationship

    The person decides what the agent may do

    The person links their account and authorizes the agent with the company: which operations, on which resource, up to what limit, until when. They can revoke it. Within that agreement the company can also start a request toward the agent.

    VXP Relationship

  2. Conditions

    The company proposes exact terms, and runs them once

    Nothing runs until one exact revision of the terms is accepted. Then it runs once, under the company's own rules. Every implementation meets the operation requirements O1 to O7.

    Operation requirements

  3. Evidence

    Each step leaves a signed statement

    The request, the terms, the approval and how it was given, the execution and what the company's system showed afterwards, each signed and linked to the one before. Anyone can check the chain.

    VXP Proof

Its own profile. VXP has a native profile that needs no other protocol: discovery on the company's domain, the agent's own identity, permission per request, operations, Proof and Relationship.

Optional interoperability. For agents that already speak Poppy or PACT, a company can also publish those adapters, on the same issuer. VXP was created by SOVRAS; anyone can implement it.
