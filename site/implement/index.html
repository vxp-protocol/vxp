From reading VXP to running it.

What you need to implement VXP on your own, then three guides, one per role. Each step shows the object that crosses the wire and the normative requirement behind it, linked to its anchor in the specification. Where the reference implementation produced the object, it is the captured one.

What is it?

An open protocol for work personal agents do for people inside companies, with two public components: VXP Proof, signed evidence checked against the company's own system, and VXP Relationship, the agreement the person authorized.

Guide 00 · On your own · native profile experimental

Without SOVRAS, without Poppy, without PACT

VXP can be implemented with its native profile alone. Nothing in VXP Proof, VXP Relationship or the operation requirements depends on Poppy or PACT, and no SOVRAS service takes part. Before the guides, three decisions.

Steps

  1. 1Native-only gateway
  2. 2Paths from the metadata
  3. 3Keys that last

Step 1 · experimental

Run a native-only gateway

  • Discovery is /.well-known/vxp.json. The issuer and its keys come from the issuer's RFC 8414 metadata, which then carries no Poppy member (poppy_domains) and an empty vxp_proof.pact.
  • A verifier anchors the proof by the issuer it knows, with no adapter document in between.
  • A token request whose resource is not the native endpoint has no other profile to go to, and gets invalid_target (RFC 8707).
  • Agent authentication uses OAuth building blocks only: a Client ID Metadata Document, private_key_jwt, DPoP and RFC 8628.

The reference implementation runs this way with both adapters off. Its test reference/test/nativo-solo.test.ts runs the whole cycle with an agent that signs with node:crypto directly, and checks the proof with the independent verifier anchored by the issuer alone. No agent outside SOVRAS has used the native profile yet.

Adapters are optional. If you add them later (Guide 01, step 9), they share the same issuer, authorization server and metadata.

Step 2

Choose your own paths, and publish them in the metadata

VXP fixes only the well-known locations, /.well-known/vxp.json and the issuer's RFC 8414 metadata, and the native endpoints under the endpoint that vxp.json declares. Every other location is discovered: the key sets, the log's checkpoint and the proof bundle are read from vxp_proof in the metadata. Those paths are your own choice and carry no meaning.

Step 3

Keep proof keys persistent, and rotate without losing old proofs

  • Statements name their key by kid, the RFC 7638 thumbprint.
  • When you rotate a proof key, keep the earlier public key in your JWKS for as long as proofs signed with it are expected to be verified. Never reuse a retired key.
  • A key removed from the JWKS no longer verifies against live material: the verifier answers unknown_key. Against material saved while the key was published, the same proof still verifies, and a verifier that uses saved material says so, with the date it was obtained.
  • Keys generated in memory and lost when the process stops make every proof they signed unverifiable, unless their public keys were saved.

With VXP_KEYS_DIR, the reference implementation keeps its operator, log and company keys across restarts, and a rotation keeps the earlier public key published. Until 22 October 2026 its keys lived in memory, which is why most proofs of 21 October can no longer be checked by signature (Examples).

SHOULD keep proof keys persistent. MUST NOT reuse a retired key.

Step 2

Publish the issuer metadata with vxp_proof and vxp_relationship

The trust anchor of everything VXP signs is the RFC 8414 metadata of the company's issuer. The paths in the capture are the reference implementation's own; yours go here, and verifiers read them from here (Guide 00, step 2). Add two parameters: vxp_proof, with the trust members (company, keys, operator, custody, log, domains, PACT faces), and vxp_relationship, with the relationship key and the actions the company may initiate.

  • company is the issuer; jwks_uri sits under the issuer (same scheme and host, path starting with the issuer's path plus /).
  • operator.jwks_uri sits under operator.issuer.
  • custody is mandatory and true: operator_hosted, customer_managed or customer_signed_binding. It is a declaration; verifiers report it and never grant a second root from it.
  • An empty log.witnesses means the level logged is out of reach, and the company does not claim otherwise.
  • Copies of these members in poppy.json or an Agent Card are identical.

MUST publish vxp_relationship.jwks_uri with a key distinct from the proof, Poppy and PACT keys (R5). company_initiated_actions bounds what any relationship may contain (R1).

Step 4

Meet the operation requirements where a request becomes an operation

Operation requirements O1 to O7 are behavior, not an interface: VXP defines no endpoint for them. Where an external request turns into an operation on your system, what passes is the intersection of what the person delegated, what the company allows and what the resource admits. The controls that enforce them inside your systems are yours; VXP Proof records the evidence that lets anyone check them afterwards.

Step 5

Freeze and sign each revision

What the person approves is a revision: immutable, numbered from 1, and only the latest can be accepted. For each revision you issue, sign a terms statement with its digest.

  • terms_digest covers {company, operation_id, revision, summary, terms, expires_at, user_approval_required}: the summary is part of what the person approves.
  • terms carries vxp_salt, at least 128 random bits, new in every revision.
  • commitment.mode comes from what the capability can execute and verify, never from what the agent asks. A capability that depends on a system you do not control is proposed conditional, and each condition is a term name.
  • read_back_terms names the terms the read-back must observe.
  • Past max_revisions the request ends failed with negotiation_limit_exceeded; an expired revision gets proposal_expired.

MUST a revision issued and not signed cannot be confirmed. The limits allow at most one automatic execution attempt per accepted revision.

Step 7

Sign what you do not know as unknown, and correct it later

  • Pending. If you do not know whether the effect happened, sign outcome.state = "unknown". Silence and failed are both wrong. Poppy sees in_progress and the operation stays open (P3).
  • Learned later. What you learn after a final statement goes as a correction on the last final statement, never as a second outcome. A correction to succeeded cites a matching read_back issued after the statement it corrects (P4).
  • Contradiction. If the read-back differs from what was approved, sign the read_back with matches_terms: false and what was observed; the result stays unknown, the operation moves to waiting_human, with no retry and no success (P5).
  • Not checkable. A capability with no read-back signs read_back.method = "none" and outcome = unknown, once. It never emits succeeded (P6).

MUST before outcome: succeeded, after the execution, the last read_back has method: "system_read_back", matches_terms: true and observes, under the same name, every term in read_back_terms. Statements are never edited or deleted.

Step 9

Add Poppy draft 0.2 and PACT 1.0 adapters on the same issuer

A company may publish vxp.json, poppy.json and the PACT Agent Card at once. The issuer, the authorization server and the metadata are the same for the three entries, and an agent that speaks Poppy or PACT needs nothing native.

  • I1. Each adapter follows the external contract literally: its objects, states, errors and discovery. Inside an adapter, VXP leaves Poppy's and PACT's discovery, authorization, operations and states exactly as they are.
  • I4. What VXP adds travels in each protocol's extension points: a domain-named member in Poppy, metadata or extensions in A2A. Never as new values of their enums. The native state reaches A2A as metadata["https://vxpprotocol.org/ext/native/v0.1"] and stays out of Poppy's state.
  • I5. Nothing of VXP enters Poppy's or PACT's catalogs: the relationship checkbox is no scope on the Agent Card.
  • I2. However many adapters, competing operations on one resource execute at most once among them. Entries produce separate operations, one per identity; the first to pass the precondition takes effect, the others end failed with no effect and with their evidence.

Keep the official suites green with VXP present: the Poppy validator and the PACT Identity suite are the check that the contract stayed literal.

Step 6

GET /v1/requests/{id} returns the native state, its projections to Poppy and A2A, the proposal, effects, cancellation, result and proof_url. GET /v1/requests/{id}/events lists every transition. A read never changes the state by itself; it may trigger reconciliation, which never repeats the action.

The four final states, succeeded, failed, cancelled and expired, never change. Later facts reach you as a correction in the proof.

Guide 03 · For independent verifiers

Check who stated what, and name the root

A verifier takes three inputs: the bundle, an anchor it knows on its own, and its policy. It returns a verdict with the levels it derived. Two conforming verifiers give the same verdict, or the same error code at the same seq, on any chain.

Step 1

Anchor the issuer from what you already know

Start from the company's domain or issuer, known by your own means. Never from a document or URL handed to you by whoever presents the bundle: anyone can publish keys on their own domain that claim to be the company.

  1. Fetch the RFC 8414 metadata of the issuer and require its issuer identical, character by character.
  2. Take the trust members from its vxp_proof: company equal to the issuer, jwks_uri under the issuer, operator.jwks_uri under operator.issuer.
  3. If you use a copy in poppy.json or an Agent Card, require it identical in those members.
  4. Any failure, including a 404, a download error or non-JSON, gives issuer_not_anchored: answer with the verdict, never crash.

Anchors per profile: poppy_domain, brand_domain, pact_issuer or issuer. The reference verifiers report key_source: issuer_metadata_live. If you verify against material saved earlier, for example after a key rotation, say so, with the date the material was obtained (key rotation).

MUST anchor before checking any signature. The anchor ties the keys to whoever controls the issuer, which can be the operator under a company domain.

Step 2

Check format and signatures

  • SD-JWT of RFC 9901 §4, last component empty, no Key Binding JWT.
  • Canonical base64url in every segment and disclosure: decode, re-encode and compare (non_canonical_encoding).
  • Header alg: ES256, typ: vxp-stmt+sd-jwt, no crit; recompute kid as the RFC 7638 thumbprint.

  • The payload bytes are exactly JCS(payload) (non_canonical_payload).
  • The kind fits the signer's role (role_mismatch).
  • Apply RFC 9901 §7.1 to the disclosures; the later rules use the processed payload.

Step 3

Check the chain, in the specified order

  • v, company, op, aud and user are equal across the chain, and aud equals the agent verifying if it declares itself.
  • seq = i, and prev is the digest of statement i − 1: "sha-256:" ‖ base64url(SHA-256(ASCII(header "." payload))), the JWS signing input without signature or disclosures.
  • After a final statement, only read_back, correction and revocation fit.
  • Semantic order per kind: one user_approval on the latest revision, company_review between approval and execution, one execution on the approved digest, one outcome on the latest terms.
  • There is an outcome; a bundle without one is treated as unknown (no_outcome).

MUST check the rules in their order, per seq. The first failure decides the code and its seq.

Step 8

State what a signature leaves open

An intact chain proves who stated what, on which exact terms, in which order, and what was observed when reading the company's system, with its root of trust named. It leaves these questions to the named root:

  • whether what was read in the company's system is true;
  • whether the approval through the channel actually happened;
  • under operator_hosted, whether the company took part at all;
  • whether another, contradictory bundle exists on the same resource: one bundle cannot show it, only a log with witnesses can.

As implemented in draft 0.1, without external witnesses and with the company key held by the operator, a VXP proof is an attestation signed by the operator: complete and ordered, and dependent on SOVRAS as operator. Write your policy in these terms, never as verified: true.

Implementation questions

Answers from the specification

Each answer comes from the specification in its revision of 23 October 2026, and links to the section it rests on.

Does a company need Poppy, PACT or the SOVRAS platform to implement VXP?

No. VXP can be implemented with the native profile alone: nothing in VXP Proof, VXP Relationship or the operation requirements depends on Poppy or PACT, and no SOVRAS service is needed. Poppy and PACT are optional adapters for agents that already speak them. The implementation available today is SOVRAS's reference implementation, under Apache 2.0, which runs as a native-only gateway in its tests; the native profile is experimental, and no agent outside SOVRAS has used it yet.

§1 Native-only gateway
My keys were generated in memory and the server restarted. Can old proofs still be checked?

Only if their public keys were saved. Against saved material, a proof still verifies, and the verifier says it used saved material, with its date. Keep proof keys persistent, and when you rotate, keep the earlier public key in your JWKS.

Key rotation
Can one company serve vxp.json, poppy.json and a PACT Agent Card at the same time?

Yes. The issuer, the authorization server and the metadata are the same for the three entries. Entries produce separate operations, one per identity, and only one can take effect on a resource.

§8 I2
How stable is the native profile?

/.well-known/vxp.json and the /v1 endpoints are experimental, and the document says so with "status": "experimental". They run the full cycle in process and over the network; adoption by a third-party agent is pending. While the protocol is a 0.x draft, every new draft is treated as incompatible with the previous one.

§8
My capability has no way to read the result back. What do I emit?

Sign read_back.method = "none" and outcome = unknown, once. Its highest result is unknown; a later correction can take it to failed, cancelled or expired, never to succeeded. It also must not announce read-back in max_assurance.

P6
The execution timed out and I do not know if it applied. Failed?

Sign outcome.state = "unknown". Poppy sees in_progress and the operation stays open. When a later read-back matches, issue a correction to succeeded citing it.

P3 P4
Is the agent saying "the user approved" enough for company_channel?

That approval is agent_asserted, and the verifier names it operator_reported_agent_approval. company_channel means the person answered from the channel the company already linked to them, with a display_code bound to the revision. In the native profile and in Poppy, approval is agent_asserted today; company_channel runs in PACT and on the company's own approval pages.

P2 Company-channel approval
Where does waiting_human appear to a Poppy or A2A agent?

Internal states stay internal. Poppy sees in_progress and A2A sees TASK_STATE_WORKING. Under A2A the internal state may travel in metadata; under Poppy it never goes in the operation's state.

E2 State projection
Can the relationship consent show up as a scope on the Agent Card?

It stays out of every Poppy and PACT catalog. The PACT delegated test caught exactly that in the reference implementation, and it was corrected.

I5
What if the person revokes while a company-initiated proposal is pending?

Accepting re-checks the relationship and the delegation. The answer is 403 relationship_revoked and nothing runs. A late reply on a revoked relationship does not count.

R9 R16
My agent has no HTTP inbox. Can it still hold a relationship?

Through a Poppy conversation, yes: the relationship is asked, received and answered inside it, as messages with data, without touching Poppy's contract. Without an open conversation, a delivery is recorded as failed. Retries with backoff are pending.

§7
Does a valid chain prove the operation was carried out?

No. It proves who stated what, on which exact terms, in which order, and what was observed when reading the company's system, with its root named. Today that root is the operator: single_root: operator, logged: false. A verdict keeps integrity, provenance, authority, declared result and corroboration apart.

§1 Reading a verdict P7
Are the vxpprotocol.org identifiers final?

They are the draft 0.1 names, renamed from the annex on 22 October 2026. vxpprotocol.org was registered that day and is under SOVRAS's control, so the identifiers use a domain the publisher controls, as Poppy requires; this site is published there. While VXP is a 0.x draft, every new draft is treated as incompatible with the previous one.
